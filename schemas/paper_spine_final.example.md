Figure 3 supports the central claim. Panel A shows representative CXCR4
immunofluorescence in CTR vs SPATS2-KD ARD cells; panel B quantifies mean
CXCR4 intensity per independent experiment (n=3 per group, unpaired Welch
t-test). The effect direction is consistent across all replicates and the
statistical unit (independent experiment) matches the declared
statistics_contract. No overclaim beyond the in-vitro system is made.
